WEB DEMO DERIVATIVES

The JPEG files retain the original camera bytes. Corresponding PNG files
are Iris RGB-normalized display copies at the original dimensions.
No crop, drawn box or retouch is baked into these images.
Source human boxes enter the demo as proposals, awaiting the visitor's
review. Saved model outputs remain separate and unchanged.
