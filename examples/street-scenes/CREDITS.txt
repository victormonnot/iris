IRIS street-scenes sample

Two photographs and all 19 of their original COCO instance annotations.
The original JPEG bytes and annotation geometry are unchanged. The sample
retains the source COCO val2017 split as "val". It is for trying import and
annotation, not a training set or an independent model-quality benchmark.
